  Yaman Recordings proudly presents “Venomous”, the latest hard-hitting release
  by CyFunk – and it's OUT NOW!


  Four tracks. Four different shades of darkness. One uncompromising sonic
  experience.


  Featuring “Venomous”, “Taste of Love”, “G4me Crush” and “Riot”, this
  electrifying release blends dark, cinematic sound design with relentless
  energy, futuristic atmospheres and raw neurofunk power.


  From the venomous intensity of the title track to the emotional depth of
  “Taste of Love”, the high-octane drive of “G4me Crush” and the unapologetic
  force of “Riot” – each track brings its own identity, coming together in one
  explosive journey of sound, aggression and emotion.


  With “Venomous”, Yaman Recordings continues to push the boundaries of modern
  electronic music, delivering a release made for the underground, the
  dancefloor and everyone who thrives on uncompromising sounds.


  THE WAIT IS OVER. THE VENOM IS RELEASED.


  “Venomous” is more than just a release. It's a statement.
