YAMAN EVENTS

HAUNTEDVILA

Z pulsu pražského undergroundu vznikly Yaman Events — noci postavené na těžkém bassu, futuristické atmosféře a nekompromisní energii. Movement zaměřený na cutting-edge eventy v Česku se posouvá do další kapitoly a rozšiřuje se za hranice danceflooru jako platforma pro forward-thinking Neurofunk a Crossbreed.Born from the underground pulse of Prague, Yaman Events has been shaping nights driven by heavy bass, futuristic atmospheres and uncompromising energy. What started as a movement focused on cutting-edge events in the Czech Republic is now evolving into the next chapter — expanding beyond the dancefloor as a label platform for forward-thinking Neurofunk and Crossbreed music.
