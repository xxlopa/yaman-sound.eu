# YAMAN SOUND — Legal pages setup

The website now contains:

- `/privacy.html` — Privacy Policy / Zásady ochrany osobních údajů
- `/terms.html` — Terms & Conditions / Obchodní podmínky

Both pages are editable in **Pages CMS → Legal / Privacy**.

## One required step before using the Privacy Policy URL in Meta Lead Ads

Open **Pages CMS → Site settings** and fill in:

1. **Legal operator / jméno nebo firma** — the real person or company operating YAMAN SOUND.
2. **IČO** — if applicable.
3. **Legal address / sídlo** — the operator/controller address.
4. **Legal / privacy contact email** — currently preset to `booking@yamansound.eu`.

The site intentionally does not invent these legal identification details.

After deployment, use this URL in Meta/Facebook Lead Ads:

`https://yamansound.eu/privacy.html`

The footer already links both legal pages, and the newsletter signup page links to the Privacy Policy as well.

The legal text is a practical template tailored to the current YAMAN SOUND site (events, ticketing, rental, merchandise, newsletter/Brevo and Meta lead forms). It should be reviewed if the legal operator, services, tracking tools, payment flow or sales process changes.
