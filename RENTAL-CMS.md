# Rental in Pages CMS

The public rental page is `/rental.html`.

Manage equipment in **Pages CMS → Rental**.

Each item has:
- Product name
- Quantity
- Category (DJ / Audio, Control / Data, Lights)
- Position/order
- Optional product photo
- Optional CZ / EN description
- Published toggle

Photos are optional. Until a photo is uploaded, the site shows a YAMAN-styled placeholder. When you add a photo in Pages CMS, it is uploaded to `assets/uploads/rental/` and appears automatically on the rental page.

Rental intro text and contact email can be changed under **Pages CMS → Site settings**.
