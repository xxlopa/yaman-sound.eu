YAMAN Recordings

Yaman Recordings je forward-thinking Drum & Bass label propojující cutting-edge producenty, DJs a vizuální stránku moderní underground rave kultury. Stojí na těžkém sound designu, nekompromisní energii a silné identitě mezi neurofunkem, crossbreedem, industriálními texturami a futuristickou bass music.

Yaman spojuje umělce, kteří staví na inovaci, technické přesnosti a syrovém dancefloor impactu — platforma, kde se hudba, vizuály a rave culture potkávají.Yaman Recordings is a forward-thinking Drum & Bass label focused on connecting cutting-edge producers, DJs and the visual side of modern underground rave culture. Built around heavy sound design, uncompromising energy and a strong artistic identity, the label moves between neurofunk, crossbreed, industrial textures and futuristic bass music while pushing a distinct audiovisual vision.

Driven by precision production, dark atmospheres and underground mentality, Yaman brings together artists who value innovation, technical skill and raw dancefloor impact — creating a platform where music, visuals and rave culture collide.
